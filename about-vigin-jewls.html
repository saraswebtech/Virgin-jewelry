About Us

Our Story of Craftsmanship & Trust

We believe every piece of jewellery tells a story — of artistry, ethics, and enduring beauty. At Virgin Jewels, we blend time-honoured craftsmanship with modern innovation to create pieces that resonate with your soul.

Virgin Jewels is a division of VirginEx India, specializing in Natural Diamonds, Lab-Grown Diamonds, Semi-Precious Stones and fine jewellery.

Based in Bangalore, India, we combine diamond sourcing, product development and jewellery craftsmanship to serve customers and business partners across international markets.

Our philosophy is simple: offer beautiful products, transparent information and dependable quality while giving customers the freedom to choose between natural and lab-grown diamonds.

QUALITY & CERTIFICATION

QUALITY YOU CAN TRUST

At Virgin Jewels, quality is not just a promise – it's a process.
Every piece of jewellery goes through multiple checks to ensure excellence you can trust.

DIAMOND CERTIFICATION

Every natural and lab-grown diamond is carefully selected and evaluated for the 4Cs – Cut, Color, Clarity and Carat Weight. We provide detailed diamond reports from trusted independent laboratories.

GOLD PURITY

Our gold jewellery is crafted in 14KT and 18KT gold to ensure lasting beauty and value. Each piece is hallmarked for purity as per industry standards.

STONE QUALITY

We source only genuine semi-precious stones that are carefully checked for color, clarity, cut and durability.

CRAFTSMANSHIP

Our skilled artisans blend traditional techniques with modern technology to create fine jewellery that is elegant, durable and timeless.

QUALITY INSPECTION

Every piece undergoes a multi-step inspection process before it reaches you, ensuring it meets our strict quality benchmarks.

OUR COMMITMENT

We are committed to delivering jewellery that is authentic, beautifully crafted and made to be cherished for generations.
