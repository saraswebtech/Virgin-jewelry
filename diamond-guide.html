NATURAL VS LAB-GROWN

Explain the differences clearly and honestly.

♧

NATURAL DIAMONDS

Formed by nature over billions of years beneath the earth's surface.

Each diamond is unique with a story as old as the planet.

♢

LAB-GROWN DIAMONDS

Created in advanced laboratories using cutting- edge technology that replicates natural processes.

They have the same physical, chemical and optical properties as natural diamonds.

Both natural and lab-grown diamonds are real diamonds.

The choice comes down to your values, preference and purpose.
